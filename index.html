把今天排清楚A plan for today

根据你的求职目标和可用时间生成每日任务，每一项都写明为什么做、做到什么程度算完成。Daily tasks built from your goals and the time you have, each with a reason and a clear finish line.

所见即所得的笔记，记录错因与改正思路，按公司或岗位一键生成复习清单。Rich notes that keep your mistakes and fixes, turned into a review list by company or role in one step.

记得，但先问你Remembers, with your OK

Principles

温柔，
但有分寸Gentle, and within bounds

数据只在本机Your data stays home

目标、笔记和聊天记录都存在你电脑上的本地数据库里。只有你发给 AI 的那条消息，会送到你自己选择的模型服务。Goals, notes and chats live in a local database on your computer. Only the message you send to the AI goes to the model service you chose.

她提建议，你做决定She suggests, the app checks

AI 不能直接改动数据，计划要先通过校验；不能读写文件，也不能执行命令。The AI cannot change data directly, and every plan is validated first. It has no file access and cannot run commands.

不会就说不会Honest when things fail

网络或模型出问题时如实告诉你，不用模板回答敷衍。查到的资料都附上来源。When the network or model fails, she says so instead of faking a reply. Anything she looks up comes with sources.

陪伴不设门槛Closeness, never a paywall

一起完成任务会让她更熟悉你，但这只改变说话的温度，从不锁住任何功能。Finishing tasks together makes her warmer over time. It changes only her tone and never locks a feature.
